Customer Segmentation Report
============================

1. Project Goal
The goal is to segment mall customers using unsupervised learning. K-Means was implemented from scratch without using any ready-made clustering algorithm.

2. Dataset Overview
Dataset shape: (200, 6)
Initial analysis includes gender count, numeric feature distributions, and the income-spending scatter plot.

3. Features Used for Final Clustering
Final clustering uses only Annual Income (k$) and Spending Score (1-100).
These two variables directly represent customer purchasing power and purchasing behavior, so they produce clear and interpretable marketing segments.
Age and Gender are still used for exploratory analysis and cluster interpretation, but not for distance calculation in the final K-Means model.

4. Preprocessing
The selected clustering features were standardized before K-Means because Euclidean distance is sensitive to feature scale.

5. K-Means Implementation
The implementation includes random centroid initialization, Euclidean distance calculation, iterative assignment and centroid update, empty-cluster handling, and multiple initializations.
Number of initializations for each K: 50

9. Business Interpretation
High income and high spending customers are valuable customers for loyalty programs and premium offers.
High income and low spending customers have purchasing power but may need personalized offers or engagement campaigns.
Low income and high spending customers may respond well to discounts and affordable bundles.
Low income and low spending customers should be targeted with basic promotions and low-risk campaigns.
Medium income and average spending customers represent stable regular customers.
